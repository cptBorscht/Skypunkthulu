A tosculi hive-stall behind a resin curtain, and the only place in Goa Zo that
sells living items. Everything grafted here runs on the living item rules
(SW p. 208): DC 12 to attach to a tosculi, DC 17 to anyone else, DC 20 to
remove — and a graft only lasts 1d4 weeks before it dies and must be replaced.

Buy the chirurgeon's tools and a jar of saliva with the graft, or you will be
back. The healing resin repairs a damaged item; nothing else does.
