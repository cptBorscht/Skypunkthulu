# Grafts, Bionics, and Attunement

How the three augmentation tracks interact at this table. Buy grafts at
[[The Chirurgeon's Hollow]]; buy bionics at [[Biomechanical Enhancination]].

## The short version

There are **three separate pools**. Filling one never eats into another.

| Pool                  | Limit                            | Time to bond               | Counts against magic items? |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Magic item attunement | 3                                | 1 short rest               | —                           |
| Bionic upgrades       | 2                                | 7 days of focused practice | No (rules as written)       |
| Tosculi grafts        | Constitution modifier, minimum 1 | None; surgery only         | No (house rule)             |

So a Con 16 character can run 3 magic items, 2 bionics, and 3 grafts at once.

> [!important] The one thing to remember
> A graft needs **living tissue**. It cannot attach to a body part that has
> been replaced by a bionic. That, not the slot count, is what forces a choice
> between the two tracks.

## What the books actually say

### Magic items

Standard 5e: three attuned items, attunement takes a short rest.

### Bionics (Airship Campaigns, p. 211)

Bionics bond "as if it were a magic item requiring attunement," but on their own
track:

- Attuning takes **7 days** of focused practice with the upgrade equipped. An
  interruption fails the attempt outright. This is not a short rest.
- A creature can be attuned to **no more than two** bionic upgrades.
- Explicitly RAW: *"Bionic upgrade attunements do not count as magic item
  attunements. Therefore, a creature can attune to three magic items and two
  bionic upgrades."*
- Attunement ends if you stop meeting prerequisites, if the upgrade is more than
  100 feet away for 24 hours, or if you die.
- An upgrade usually **replaces a body part**, is built for one specific
  creature, and is not transferable. Use common sense on duplicates: you cannot
  normally have three bionic arms.

### Tosculi living items (Southlands Worldbook, p. 208)

Living items **require no attunement at all** - not one of them carries an
attunement line. They are gated by surgery and by rot instead.

| Action | Check |
|--------|-------|
| Attach to a tosculi | DC 12 Dexterity (Medicine) |
| Attach to a non-tosculi | DC 17 Dexterity (Medicine) |
| Attach it to yourself | As above, with disadvantage |
| Remove any graft | DC 20 Dexterity (Medicine) |
| Remove one from yourself | As above, with disadvantage |

- Proficiency with **tosculi chirurgeon's tools** grants advantage on attach and
  remove checks.
- A **failed removal** leaves the item in place, and that creature cannot be the
  subject of another removal attempt until it finishes a long rest.
- **Fail by 5 or more** and the graft is damaged. It stops working until repaired.
- **Repair** only works on a removed graft: 8 hours and a DC 20 Wisdom check with
  chirurgeon's tools, or simply smear on **tosculi healing resin**. A failed check
  destroys the graft instead.
- **Tosculi saliva** lets you detach a graft from a helpless, willing, or dead
  creature with no check at all. A jar holds 1d6 + 1 applications.
- A living item lasts **1d4 weeks**, then stops functioning and must be replaced.

## House rules

Four calls, because the books do not answer these.

### 1. Grafts have their own slots

Grafts do not consume magic item attunement (already RAW, since they require no
attunement) and they **do not consume bionic slots** either. They are grown
tissue, not eldritch-crystal machinery, and the two subsystems have nothing to do
with each other.

Your graft limit is your **Constitution modifier, minimum 1**. Your body's
tolerance for foreign tissue is a physical trait, so the barbarian carries more
hardware than the wizard.

> [!tip] Simpler alternative
> If tracking a variable number annoys you, use a flat **2 grafts**, mirroring
> the bionic cap. Nothing else in this note changes.

### 2. Grafts need living tissue

A graft cannot attach to a bionic body part. Grafts root into veins, nerves, and
bone; a bionic limb has none of those.

| Graft | Location | Blocked by |
|-------|----------|------------|
| Tosculi Lenses | Eyes | Bionic Eye, Eldritch Blast Eye |
| Stunted Wing Graft | Back | Bionic Wings |
| Tosculi Spur | Forearm and hand | Bionic Arm or Hand, Bladed Hands, Blaster Arm, Ensnaring Arm |
| Tosculi Carapace | Chest | Bionic Organs |
| Tosculi Antennae | Forehead | Nothing |
| Tosculi Pheromone Glands | Collarbones | Nothing |

Two grafts in the same location never stack either. One set of lenses, one
carapace.

### 3. Grafts rot on a schedule, and the schedule is public

Roll the **1d4 weeks openly** when the graft goes in, and write the expiry date
on the character sheet using the **Skyaian calendar**. A graft dying mid-dungeon
because nobody tracked it is a feel-bad; a graft the party knows expires in nine
days is a clock, and clocks are good.

> [!question] Ambiguity, resolved
> The book says non-tosculi "can't keep them on indefinitely," then separately
> gives every living item a 1d4 week lifespan. **Our ruling: tosculi hosts
> sustain grafts indefinitely; everyone else gets 1d4 weeks.** That reads both
> sentences as saying something, and gives tosculi characters a real perk.
> Flip it to "1d4 weeks for everyone" if grafts start feeling too permanent.

### 4. A dead graft still occupies the slot

Expired and damaged grafts do not fall off. The dead tissue holds its slot and
its body location until someone makes the DC 20 removal check, so replacing a
graft in the field means cutting the old one out first.  Subtract a 1d4 from DEX based roles if desired.

## Player reference: getting a graft

1. **Buy it** at [[The Chirurgeon's Hollow]]. Uncommon grafts run about 360 gl,
   rare ones about 3,600 gl.
2. **Have it installed.** A hive chirurgeon does it on a DC 17 check with
   advantage from their tools. Doing it yourself, in the field, without tools, is
   a DC 17 with disadvantage and is how people lose grafts.
3. **Kit you want anyway:** chirurgeon's tools (60 gl, and the proficiency to use
   them), a jar of saliva (240 gl) for clean removal, and healing resin (360 gl),
   which is the only no-roll way to repair a damaged graft.
4. **Write down the expiry date.** See house rule 3.

> [!warning] The failure everyone hits first
> Botching a removal by 5 or more damages the graft, and a damaged graft cannot
> be repaired while it is still attached. You have to get it out first - and you
> cannot retry the removal until the wearer finishes a long rest.
