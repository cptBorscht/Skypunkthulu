# Fishing Minigame

A downtime minigame by **Saffy Penrose**. Jump to your [[#Catch log]] to see what
you still need for **Catch 'Em All**.

## How to fish

One run of these steps = **one whole day** of fishing.

You are proficient with fisher's supplies, so all the numbers below already
assume that.

> [!tip] The rule people get wrong
> **Times first. Plus second.**
> Multiply for a pattern, then add your gear bonus. Never the other way round.

### My numbers

|               |         |
| ------------- | ------- |
| Dice I roll   | **5d6** |
| Rerolls I get | 7       |
| My gear bonus | 0       |


### Step 1. Roll

Roll **5d6**.

Later on this grows:

- Angler: roll **6d6**
- Master Angler: roll **7d6**

### Step 2. Reroll

You may reroll dice you do not like.

- The number of rerolls is on your card above.
- You may reroll the **same die** more than once.
- Put a d10 next to you and count it down. That way you never lose track.

### Step 3. Add

Add up all your dice.

This is your **base score**.

### Step 4. Multiply

Look at your final dice. Do they make a pattern?

| Pattern | What it means | Multiply by |
|---|---|---|
| Full House | three the same, plus two the same | **x2** |
| Four-of-a-Kind | four dice the same | **x3** |
| Straight | five in a row, like 1-2-3-4-5 | **x4** |
| Five-of-a-Kind | five dice the same | **x5** |

You do not have to use it. Only take it if it helps.

No pattern? Skip this step.

### Step 5. Add your gear

Now add your flat bonus.

- Improved Pole: **+3**
- Improved Line: **+1**
- Improved Lures: **+1**
- Angler: **+10**
- Master Angler: another **+10**

All of these stack.

### Step 6. Look it up

Take your final number to the fishing table below.

Pick the **freshwater** or **saltwater** column, depending on where you fished.

> [!note] Score of 60 or more?
> That fish may be magical. Ask your DM what it can do.

### A worked example

```
Roll 5d6 ......  4  4  4  2  1
Reroll 2 and 1   (you have 4 rerolls, you used 2)
New dice ......  4  4  4  4  6

Add ...........  22
Pattern .......  four 4s = Four-of-a-Kind = x3
Multiply ......  22 x 3 = 66
Add gear ......  66 + 5 = 71

Your score ....  71
You caught ....  Scorpion carp  (or Mightfish in salt water)
```

71 is over 60, so ask your DM if it is magical.

> [!question] With 6 or 7 dice, what counts as a pattern?
> You only need the dice named in the table. A Straight still means five in a
> row, even if you rolled seven dice. Your spare dice still add to the sum.

## Equipment

Bonuses from gear are **flat and stack**, and all apply **after** any multiplier.
Kitted out with all three, you are at **+5**.

| Item | Cost | Weight | Effect |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| Fisher's supplies | 5 gp | 8 lb. | Artisan's tools: pole, tackle box, line, hooks, bait, bobbers, lures. Proficiency improves your rerolls. |
| Improved Pole | 12 gp | 2 lb. | Reinforced pole. **+3** to your overall score. |
| Improved Line | 5 gp | - | Mithril-infused line for tougher fish. **+1** to your overall score. |
| Improved Lures | 8 gp | 1 lb. | Vibrant lures that draw elusive fish. **+1** to your overall score. |

## Advancements

| Advancement | Requirement | Benefit |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| **Angler** | 30+ in-game days fishing | Roll an extra d6 (6d6 total). May also take **+10** to your overall score, after multipliers. |
| **Master Angler** | 100+ in-game days fishing | Roll an extra d6 (7d6 total). May also take **another +10**, after multipliers. Stacks with Angler's bonus. |
| **Catch 'Em All** | Catch every fish in the table at least once (junk and treasure excluded) | A sea nymph gifts you the **Lucky Hat**. |

### Lucky Hat

Wondrous item, artifact. Requires attunement by a Master Angler.

A worn brown leather hat. On attuning, you may change its form to anything you
like, so long as it is still a hat. While attuned:

- You cannot be blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, petrified, or stunned
  against your will.
- You are immune to disease, and you cease to age.
- Whenever you fish, you **catch twice as many fish** as you normally would.

The wearer feels a strong, though never urgent, desire to smoke a pipe or chew on
a good piece of straw.

# Catch log

## Days fished

Track toward Angler (30 days) and Master Angler (100 days).

| Total days fished | Angler | Master Angler |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | [ ] | [ ] |

Tick a fish the first time you land it. Junk and treasure do not count toward
**Catch 'Em All** - you need all **52** species below.

```dataviewjs
const raw = dv.current().file.tasks;
const tasks = Array.isArray(raw) ? raw : (raw && Array.isArray(raw.values) ? raw.values : []);
const heading = (t) => String((t.header && t.header.subpath) || (t.section && t.section.subpath) || "");
const bar = (d, n) => {
  const w = 14, f = n ? Math.round((d / n) * w) : 0;
  return "|".repeat(f) + ".".repeat(w - f) + "  " + (n ? Math.round((d / n) * 100) : 0) + "%";
};
const rows = [];
let done = 0, total = 0;
for (const pool of ["Freshwater", "Aethersea"]) {
  const t = tasks.filter((x) => heading(x).includes(pool));
  const d = t.filter((x) => x.completed).length;
  rows.push([pool, d + " / " + t.length, t.length - d, bar(d, t.length)]);
  done += d; total += t.length;
}
rows.push(["**Total**", "**" + done + " / " + total + "**", total - done, bar(done, total)]);
dv.table(["Pool", "Caught", "Left", "Progress"], rows);
if (total > 0 && done === total) {
  dv.paragraph("**Catch 'Em All complete.** Find the sea nymph and claim the Lucky Hat.");
}
```

> [!tip] Making this note easier to read
> These are Obsidian settings, not house rules. Change them for yourself only.
>
> - **Settings > Appearance > Font**: pick a sans-serif face. Verdana, Tahoma,
>   Trebuchet MS and Open Sans are all easier to track than a serif font.
> - **Settings > Appearance > Font size**: nudge it up. 16 to 18 is a good range.
> - **Settings > Editor > Readable line length**: leave this **on**. Short lines
>   are much easier to follow back to the left margin.
> - **Settings > Appearance > Theme**: a light or sepia background with dark grey
>   text is gentler than pure black on pure white.
